Return Policy

Easy, clear and designed around a smooth NOVA after-purchase experience.

NOVACARE / 2026
01

Return window

Most eligible NOVA footwear can be requested for return within 30 days of delivery, provided the pair is unworn, clean, and returned with its original packaging and accessories.

02

Condition matters

  • Shoes must be unworn indoors and free from marks, dirt, washing, alterations or damage. Original box, tags and included accessories should be kept intact.
03

How to request a return

Open the Contact page and choose “Return & Refund”. Include your order number, the item you want to return, and the reason. Our team will reply with the next steps.

04

Inspection & outcome

Once the returned item reaches NOVA, it is inspected. Approved returns are processed according to the original payment method or the agreed store-credit option.

05

Non-returnable situations

  • Worn or visibly used footwear
  • Items damaged after delivery by misuse
  • Pairs returned without required original packaging where packaging is essential
  • Items outside the stated return window unless NOVA has approved an exception
