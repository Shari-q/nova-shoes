Cancellation Policy

A simple guide for requesting an order cancellation before your NOVA pair moves into fulfillment.

NOVACARE / 2026
01

Request quickly

Cancellation requests should be sent as soon as possible after placing an order. Once an order has entered fulfillment or shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

02

What to include

Send your order number, customer name and the reason for cancellation through the Contact page. This helps the team identify the correct order quickly.

03

If the order already shipped

If a cancellation can no longer be completed because the parcel has shipped, customer care can guide you through the applicable return process instead.

04

Payment handling

If a cancellation is approved after payment, any applicable refund is processed according to the payment method and NOVA refund process.

05

Contact NOVA

Choose “Cancellation” in the contact form and include your order number. The customer-care team will confirm the available next step.
