01 · THE BEGINNINGMore than shoes.

We design a feeling

NOVA started with a simple belief: footwear should carry the same level of thought as the clothes, spaces and objects around it. That means strong silhouettes, tactile surfaces, balanced proportions and details that become visible the closer you look.

Our collections move between everyday lifestyle, performance and limited releases — but the standard stays the same: purposeful design without unnecessary noise.
