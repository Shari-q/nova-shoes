Refund Policy

A transparent guide to how approved NOVA refunds are reviewed and processed.

NOVACARE / 2026
01

Eligibility

Refunds are issued for eligible orders after the returned item has been received and approved following inspection.

02

Original payment method

Where practical, refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time can vary by bank, wallet or payment provider after NOVA releases the refund.

03

Shipping & deductions

Any shipping or handling amount that is explicitly non-refundable will be communicated before the refund is finalized. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact NOVA promptly with order details.

04

Refund updates

If you have already returned an item and want an update, contact customer care with your order number. We can use that information to locate the latest status available to our team.

05

Need help?

For a refund question, use the Contact NOVA form and select “Return & Refund”.
